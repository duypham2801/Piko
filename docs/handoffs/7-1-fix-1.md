## Context

7-1 (`d0dd31a`, `0488519` on `feat/7-1-navigation-shell`) added `AppNav`, `AppShell` and `Screen`. The architect reviewed it in headless Chrome at 360/768/1024/1280.

**What works:**
- `make check` passes (domain 48, api 41).
- The top bar shows below `64rem` and the sidebar from `64rem`. The sidebar stays in place on scroll.
- `aria-current` is set on `/history`.
- Focus mode hides the nav on `/presets/:slug/open`, `/decisions/:id/open` and the builder's `?view=case`.
- **Sharing** still works: dialog, `Esc`, and focus back on "Chia sẻ".
- **`/s/:id`** has no nav, sends only the public GET and sets no cookie.
- The back link is at the same position on every shell screen.

**What is wrong:**
- **R1 (MUST):** the `Screen` content column stretches to fill the screen.
  - `.content` sits in the `minmax(0, 1fr)` row with the default `align-self: stretch`. Its own grid then spreads its rows over the full height.
  - On `/history` at 1280 px:
    - the title, "Đã chọn" and the empty state are spread over the whole height
    - "Về trang chủ" is about 100 px tall
  - The public `/s/:id` page has a large empty band between the strip and the buttons.
- **R2 (MUST):** `align="center"` does not centre when there is no back link.
  - Without the `.top` row, `.content` lands in the first (`auto`) row.
  - So NotFound, the decision load state and the public loading/error/unavailable pages sit at the top instead of the middle.
- **R3 (SHOULD):** the nav's DOM order is "+ Tạo", "Lịch sử", but below `64rem` the CSS `order` shows "Lịch sử" first.
  - Tab order then jumps right-to-left (`+ Tạo` at x 267, then `Lịch sử` at x 195), against the visual order (WCAG 1.3.2 / 2.4.3).
- **R4 (SHOULD):** leftover CSS:
  - **`AppNav`:**
    - `overflow-x: hidden` hides overflow instead of preventing it
    - the desktop block repeats `display: flex`
  - **Page `.content` rules repeat `Screen` defaults:**
    - CaseOpening `gap: var(--space-6)`
    - DecisionPreview `.content { gap: var(--space-6) }`, which is the whole rule
    - SharedCasePage `min-width: 0` and `gap: var(--space-6)`
- **B1 (owner decision):** Home now shows "PIKO" twice: the nav logo, and Home's large wordmark with the tagline.
  - The owner chose: **remove the large PIKO from Home, and move the tagline under the small logo in the nav.**

## Requirements

### R1 + R2 — `Screen` rows
In `Screen.module.css`:
- **`.content`:** add `grid-row: 2` and `align-self: start`.
  - The content always sits in the `1fr` row, with or without the back link.
  - Its height is its own content, not the row height.
- **`.center .content`:** keeps `align-self: center`.
- The empty first row has no height when there is no back link, so nothing else changes.

### R3 — One nav order
- Remove both `order` declarations from `AppNav.module.css`.
- Render the links in the DOM as **"Lịch sử" first, then "+ Tạo"**, at every width.
  - The mobile top bar keeps its current look: "+ Tạo" at the far right.
  - The sidebar shows the logo, "Lịch sử", then the "+ Tạo" pill. 7-2 places "Của bạn" right under the pill.
- Tab order must equal visual order at 360 and 1280 px.

### R4 — Leftover CSS
- **`AppNav.module.css`:**
  - Remove `overflow-x: hidden`. The bar must still have no horizontal scroll at 360 px, because the content fits.
  - Remove the repeated `display: flex` in the `64rem` block.
- **`CaseOpening.module.css`:** remove `gap: var(--space-6)` from `.content`; keep `justify-items: center`.
- **`DecisionPreview`:**
  - Delete the `.content` rule.
  - Stop passing `className={styles.content}` to `Screen`.
- **`SharedCasePage.module.css`:** remove `min-width: 0` and `gap: var(--space-6)` from `.content`; keep `justify-items` and `text-align`.

### B1 — Brand in the nav, not on Home
**`HomePage`:**
- Remove the `<header className={styles.brand}>` with the wordmark and the tagline.
- Delete the now-unused CSS: `.wordmark`, `.tagline`, and `.brand` from the `.brand, .intro` selector.
- The page starts with the question section. The `<title>` stays `PIKO — Pick. Open. Go.`.

**`AppNav` logo:** the `Link` to `/` contains two lines:
- the wordmark `t('title')`, as now
- under it, the tagline `t('tagline')`:
  - `--text-sm`, `--color-text-muted`, body font, regular weight, `--leading-tight`
  - no wrapping (`white-space: nowrap`)

Layout:
- The logo becomes a small grid with no gap or `--space-1`, aligned to the start.
- Keep `min-height: var(--tap-target-min)`.
- **Top bar:** the logo block stays left, and the links stay vertically centred against it. The bar must fit at 360 px with no horizontal scroll.
- **Sidebar:** the same block sits at the top.
- The tagline shows on every shell screen, not only Home. Focus mode still hides the whole nav.
