## Context

7-3-fix-1 (`e630769` on `feat/7-3-case-overlay`) was verified in headless Chrome with real mouse input:
- **Closing the share dialog**, with `Esc` or "Đóng", now closes only that dialog. The case overlay stays open with its result, and focus returns to "Chia sẻ".

One item is not fixed: **F3, the desktop height.** At 1280×800 the overlay is still 736 px tall, while its content is 498 px (ready) or 622 px (revealed).
- `.dialog` keeps `inset: 0` from the phone rule.
- With `top: 0; bottom: 0`, `height: auto` stretches the element to the full inset box, and `max-height` then clamps it.

The architect tried `height: fit-content` in the browser:
- The dialog becomes 568 px, centred (top 116 px).
- In a 1280×500 window it clamps to `max-height` and scrolls inside (562/430).
