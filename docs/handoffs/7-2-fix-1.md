## Context

7-2 (the four implementer commits on `feat/7-2-sidebar-lists`) added `useMediaQuery`, the list reload keys, `NavLists` and the desktop Home. The architect reviewed it in headless Chrome at 360/1024/1280.

**What works:**
- **Requests:**
  - At 1280 px, `/` makes one `GET /api/decisions` and one `GET /api/history?limit=5`, both from the sidebar.
  - Home renders only "Chọn nhanh" sections.
  - At 360 px the nav renders no lists and Home keeps "Của bạn".
- **Resizing** across `64rem` switches the layout live.
- **The active row** has `aria-current`.
- **Reloads:**
  - `?off=` toggles make no list request.
  - After "Đi thôi" → "Trở về", the new entry is first in "Gần đây", and the sidebar did not empty while reloading.
  - A deleted decision disappears after the redirect.
- `make check` passes (domain 48, api 41).

**What is wrong:**
- **F1 (MUST):** with many saved decisions, "Của bạn" shows a **horizontal scrollbar**, and the "Của bạn" heading scrolls away with the rows.
  - `.list` is a grid with an `auto` column. Each `<li>` then takes its row's max-content width (the label is `nowrap`), so a long title overflows instead of being truncated.
  - The scroll container is the whole section, heading included.
- **F2 (SHOULD):** lint warns `react-refresh/only-export-components`, because `HistoryList.tsx` now also exports `historyEntryHref`.
  - This came from the 7-2 handoff; the helper belongs in its own module.
- **F3 (SHOULD):** `NavLists.module.css` repeats what `global.css` already resets, and mixes two heading selectors:
  - `margin: 0` on headings, `p` and lists; `padding: 0` and `list-style: none` on `ul[class]`
  - `display: block` on flex/grid children
  - `.heading` and `.sectionHeader h2`, which carry the same rules
- **F4 (SHOULD):**
  - The sidebar decision `NavLink` repeats its visible text in `aria-label`.
  - `useMediaQuery` passes a server snapshot, which this client-only SPA never uses.

## Requirements

### F1 — "Của bạn" overflow
**Truncation:** `.list` uses `grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr)`. Rows then keep the sidebar width and long titles end in "…". This applies to both lists.

**Scroll the list, not the section:**
- The "Của bạn" section is a flex column with `flex: 1 1 auto; min-height: 0`.
- Its heading stays fixed.
- Its `<ul>` gets `min-height: 0; overflow-y: auto`.
- The section itself no longer scrolls.

**States:** the empty and error lines still show under the heading.

**Result:** no horizontal scrollbar anywhere in the sidebar at 1024 and 1280 px, even with a title near the 60-character limit.

### F2 — `historyEntryHref`
- Move `historyEntryHref` unchanged into `features/history/historyEntryHref.ts` as a named export.
- `HistoryList.tsx` and `NavLists.tsx` import it from there. `HistoryList.tsx` exports only its component again.
- `make check` shows **no** lint warning.

### F3 — `NavLists.module.css`
**Remove the declarations that `global.css` already provides:**
- `margin: 0` on `.heading`, `.muted` and `.list`
- `padding: 0` and `list-style: none` on `.list`

**Remove `display: block`** from `.label` and `.meta`. They are flex/grid items, so they are blockified anyway. Keep the ellipsis rules.

**Headings:**
- Use the single `.heading` class on **both** `<h2>` elements.
- Delete the `.sectionHeader h2` selectors. The heading in the flex header keeps `min-width: 0` through `.heading`.
- Keep the bottom spacing:
  - In "Gần đây", put it on `.sectionHeader` (`margin-bottom: var(--space-2)`) and give that `<h2>` no margin.
  - Or use `gap` on the section. Pick one approach and use it for both sections.

### F4 — Small removals
- `NavLists`: remove `aria-label={decision.title}` from the sidebar decision `NavLink`. Its accessible name is already the title (the emoji is `aria-hidden`).
- `useMediaQuery`: call `useSyncExternalStore(subscribe, getSnapshot)` without the third argument.
